About Me

I am a M.Sc. student in Electrical Engineering at the University of Stuttgart, Germany.

My research interests focus on robust and intelligent control methods for robotic systems, with emphasis on nonlinear adaptive control, neural-network-based compensation, and autonomous systems.

Currently, I am working on adaptive control of robotic manipulators by combining computed torque control, Lyapunov-based adaptive methods, and RBF neural networks.

Research Interests

Robotic Control Systems

Dynamics modeling, trajectory tracking, and torque control of robotic manipulators.

Nonlinear Adaptive Control

Lyapunov-based stability analysis, adaptive laws, uncertainty estimation, and robust control.

Learning-based Control

Neural network approximation, RBF adaptive compensation, and intelligent control.

Selected Projects

Franka Gazebo RBF Adaptive Control

CTC control with online RBF neural-network adaptive compensation for robust trajectory tracking of a 7-DOF Franka FR3 manipulator under model uncertainty.

Implemented with:
ROS 2 Jazzy, Gazebo Sim, ros2_control, Pinocchio, C++ and Python.

GitHub Repository

RBF Compensation Robot Control

Adaptive RBF neural-network control framework for uncertain multi-joint robotic manipulators.

Includes:
Lyapunov stability analysis, σ-modification adaptive law, and nonlinear disturbance compensation.

GitHub Repository

DARA Lipschitz Continuous

Distributed Adaptive Resource Allocation for multi-agent systems over directed graphs.

Designed for systems with Lipschitz continuous local cost functions.
